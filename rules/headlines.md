# Chispa DE · Headlines

The 38 headline patterns of the master [headlines.md](../../../Playbook/headlines.md), a German line for each. The original ad and why each works are in the master file. A headline is the first line: the big text on a post, the hook on a reel from frame 0, the line before „mehr“ in a caption. For reel openers, [hooks.md](hooks.md) comes first. *(only if true)* lines wait for a real fact.
