# Launch kit Germany · the texts

Every German text for Chispa Studio's Instagram and Facebook in Germany, ready to copy: profile, highlights, the first 9 posts with their slides and captions. The Israeli model is `Project Plan/12-launch-kit.md`. Images are drawn on `source/board.html` and exported to `instagram/` and `facebook/` (see `README.md`).

**Every German line waits for the partner's native check.** Mark a line ✓ here once the partner has read it.

## Rules for these texts

- **du** everywhere on Instagram and Facebook. Plain warm German, only the loanwords everyone uses (Post, Story, Reel, Content). The humour is dry and aimed at agency habits, never at the client.
- **No colons or dashes on screen or in captions.** Commas and periods. A hyphen inside a compound (Chispa-Team) is fine.
- **No number of days.** Always „in wenigen Werktagen“.
- **No promised results.** No „mehr Kunden“, „mehr Reichweite“, „garantiert“, no promised hires in the Handwerk posts, no skin results in the Kosmetik post.
- **Say AI openly, never as the pitch.** Captions say „mit KI gemacht, von uns geprüft“. Every slide with a realistic AI photo carries the label pill „Mit KI erstellt“ (its final wording comes with the legal pages, step 5), and the post gets Instagram's own AI label switched on when posting (Erweiterte Einstellungen, KI‑Info).
- **No „Datei“ or „ZIP“.** Say what the client gets.
- **No „am beliebtesten“ for the Chispa package in Germany.** It isn't true yet (no sales). It says „unsere Empfehlung“ until it is.
- **Prices** as 399 €, 799 €, 1.599 €. Whether „Endpreis“ may be said waits for the Steuerberater (Kleinunternehmer). Until then the price lines say „einmal“, never „inkl. MwSt.“ or „Endpreis“.
- **Demo posts are fictional businesses,** always tagged „Beispiel“, with no real person's face. Hands, tools, details and backs of heads only.
- **Hashtags** go after two lines with a single dot, as in Israel. Five to seven, German. **No local tags or places:** the content speaks to all of Germany, not Hamm (the founder, 2026-10-22).
