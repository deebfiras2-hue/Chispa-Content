# Chispa Studio DE · Content strategy

Every weekly batch follows this file. Rules and wording limits stay in `CHISPA-RULES.md` and `claims.md`; this file says **what** to make and **why**.

## 1. Goal

Inbound conversations on WhatsApp from German small-business owners: Friseur/Barbier, Kosmetik, Café, Bäckerei, Imbiss, Laden, Handwerk, Werkstatt. Nobody shows a face, so trust comes from useful tips, specifics, the visible process and plain, honest German.

## 2. How Instagram ranks (Mosseri's public signals, 2025/26)

- **Watch time and completion** carry the most weight for reels.
- **Sends per reach** count most for reaching non-followers. **Likes per reach** count for followers.
- Reels bring reach and shares; **carousels bring saves**.
- Most views come in the **first 3 days**. Posting regularly matters more than the exact time.

What we do about it:
- **Hook in the first 1.5 s**: a contrast (two shops), a question, or a scene owners know. The hook text is on screen at frame 0.
- **Reels run 20–35 s**. Open a loop early ("Drei Dinge …", "Wer bekommt die Kundin?") and close it at the end.
- **A reason to send it** on about half the value reels: „Schick das einem Inhaber, der …“ (spoken before the sign-off; it asks for a share, not a sale).
- **Carousels are made to be SAVED**: checklists, step-by-steps, before/after. Every slide is useful on its own. The last slide says „Speicher dir das.“

## 3. Psychology toolkit (honest use only)

| Lever | How we use it |
|---|---|
| A-vs-B contrast | Two shops, same street, same prices. One shows up, one doesn't. The viewer picks a side without being told |
| Loss aversion | The customer goes next door. We show the loss, we never promise the gain |
| Recognition | The owner's own excuse („nach Feierabend“, „ab Montag“, „ich hab keine Zeit“) |
| Specificity | Real steps, real prices, real package contents. Concrete beats clever |
| Price anchoring | Market retainers of about €800–1,500 a month (claims.md, market guides) next to „einmal zahlen“. Never name an agency |
| Middle package | Chispa 699 € is „Unsere Empfehlung“ (never „Bestseller“) |
| Foot in the door | Prueba 149 € „zum Ausprobieren“, once per customer |
| Open loops (Zeigarnik) | Announce the list or the answer early and pay it off late, which keeps people watching |
| Transparency about AI | „Mit KI gemacht, von Menschen geprüft.“ 60% say AI transparency raises their trust (ARD Media, claims.md) |

## 4. The German market

- Owners distrust hype, agency jargon (Leads, Funnel), Abo-Fallen and minimum terms. They want clear prices and plain German.
- Instagram is the most used of the big platforms: 40% of people 14+ use it weekly (ARD/ZDF, as quoted in claims.md).
- Calm, firm and specific beats loud. No promised results, no fake clients, no false urgency.

## 5. Weekly mix: 7 reels + 2–3 carousels

**Reels by purpose:** 5 VALUE · 1 PROOF · 1 OFFER. Value reels end only on the sign-off. Proof and offer reels end with „Schreib uns auf WhatsApp.“ before the sign-off.

**Reels by style (rotate):**

| Style | Per week | Engine |
|---|---|---|
| LINEART A-vs-B story | 2 | reel-v2 `lineart` |
| 3D (phone, store, tiles, tags, text) | 2 | reel-v2 `3d-*` scenes |
| KINETIC Swiss typography (like reels 02/15) | 1 | reel-v2 `kin-*` scenes |
| CHARACTER (a talking shop, phone or till, like reel 17) | 1 every 1–2 weeks | reel-v2 (planned) |
| PROCESS / PROOF | 1 | any mode |

**Industries:** rotate, never the same one twice in a row. Never Ärzte, Physio, Steuerberater or Anwälte.
**Topics:** never repeat a topic from `existing-reels-series-01.md` or an earlier batch one-to-one.

## 6. Posting

- **One reel every day at 19:00 Berlin time.**
- **Carousels Tue, Thu and Sun at 12:00 Berlin time.** Images must be JPEG.
- `plan/calendar.json` lists every item. The owner approves each day the day before in `approvals/YYYY-MM-DD.txt` (see `approvals/README.md`). Nothing goes out without a „JA“.

## 7. Lead path (inbound only, § 7 UWG)

- Now: offer and proof content says „Schreib uns auf WhatsApp“ (link in bio). We reply only to people who wrote first.
- Later: a comment keyword triggers a DM (needs more Instagram API permissions). Still inbound, because the viewer asked first.
- Never cold DMs, WhatsApps or emails.

## 8. KPIs (review weekly, every Monday)

Views · average watch time / completion · shares (sends) · saves · profile visits · WhatsApp chats started.
Keep what earns watch time and sends. Cut styles or hooks that lose people in the first 3 seconds. No vanity targets in public content.
