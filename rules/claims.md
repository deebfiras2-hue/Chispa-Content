## Market facts we may quote (with the source)

Use only these, only as written, and keep the source with the piece (in its README). All from [../Research/report.md](../Research/report.md):

- Small-business agency packages usually run monthly with minimum terms of 3, 6 or 12 months; some renew automatically for 12 months.
- Typical small-business retainers cost around €800 to €1,500 a month (market guides).
- Instagram reaches 40% of people 14+ in Germany each week, ahead of Facebook at 31% and TikTok at 20% (ARD/ZDF 2025).
- 46% of retailers with a social profile find it hard to post regularly (Bitkom).
- More than 16,000 Handwerk training places stayed unfilled in 2025 (DHZ).
- 77% find labelling of AI ads important; 60% say transparency about AI raises their trust (ARD Media).

Never: a named agency or its price in a post (§ 6 UWG), or a figure rounded up to sound bigger.

## The offer and how it is sold

- **B2B only.** Chispa sells to businesses. Before paying, the buyer gives the company name and confirms „Ich bestelle als Unternehmer“ (BGH I ZR 60/16). This removes consumer withdrawal rights and allows payment up front.
- **Payment up front, by bank transfer, against a proper invoice.** No cash.
- **Inbound only** (§ 7 UWG). The partner answers people who wrote first, or who commented asking for something. No cold WhatsApp, DM, email or SMS to a business, ever. Whether he may walk into a shop with a sample waits for a lawyer.
- **No money between Israel and Germany** before the Steuerberater sets the structure.
- **Usage rights** are granted „soweit Schutzrechte bestehen“ (AI output is often not protected); never promise exclusivity.
- **Sie** in the offer, the invoice and any contract; du everywhere else.

## Labels and disclosures

- **„Mit KI erstellt“** on every slide or frame with a realistic AI person, place, object or event, at first exposure, plus Instagram's AI label switched on when posting. AI voices: the caption says so, and a reel with an AI voice carries the label.
- **Not needed** on stylised graphics, illustrations and kinetic type drawn in code (the research's reading; a lawyer confirms the AGB wording).
- **A real product before an AI background:** disputed among German lawyers. Label it.
- **„Beispiel“** on every demo post for a fictional business.
- **Music:** licensed or none. Never Instagram's library on a business reel.

## Words to avoid

| Avoid | Why | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| „garantiert“, „100%“, „immer“, „nie wieder“ (about results) | Promises a result | Say what is delivered |
| „kostenlos“, „gratis“ | Nothing is; a free post invites the wrong buyers | — |
| „Jetzt zuschlagen“, „Nur heute“, „Nur noch 3 Plätze“ | False urgency (§ 5 UWG) unless literally true | A real date, or no urgency |
| „Agentur“ about us | We are the alternative to agency habits | „Studio“ |
| „Leads“, „Funnel“, „Growth“, „Conversion“ | Agency English the reader distrusts | Plain German, or a joke about the word |
| „Datei“, „ZIP“, „Download“ | Say what the client gets | „deine Posts“, „dein Paket“ |
| „Endpreis“, „inkl. MwSt.“ | Waits for the Steuerberater | „einmal“ |
| „heilen“, „behandeln“ (medical sense), „Arzt“ for a Kosmetikerin | HWG | Cosmetic words only |
